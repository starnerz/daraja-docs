breaks in production as well as what works in sandbox.

## Then keep it running

Taking one payment is the easy half. These guides cover the part that decides
whether your books are right a month later:

- [Reconciling payments](../guides/reconciliation/) — find the payments
  whose callbacks never arrived, and prove the ones that did.
- [Handling callbacks](../guides/callbacks/) — the routes, the events, and
  why a callback is not a record.
- [Going live](../guides/going-live/) — what Safaricom requires before real
  money moves.
